Bariwala: Rental & Property Manager

B2B Property SaaS Landlord & Rental Ledger Flutter & Material 3 SQLite + Encrypted Drive Backup

An offline-first rental property management application crafted for independent flat owners, multi-unit building landlords, and real estate operators. Centralizes building and unit inventories, tenant and household profiles, automated monthly rent and utility billing, partial and advance payment allocations, recoverable maintenance expenses, and AES-256-GCM encrypted Google Drive backups.

VIEW ON GitHub Deploy for Business

Core Operational Architecture & Capabilities

Properties & Multi-Unit Inventory

Manage standalone flats and multi-story residential buildings from one unified portfolio. Track unit floor levels, bedroom/bathroom specs, square footage, configurable rent due dates, and real-time occupancy states.

Tenants, Household & Agreements

Maintain complete primary tenant KYC records (NID, phone, emergency contacts), household family occupants, and rental agreement lifecycles with isolated security deposit and advance rent accounting.

Automated Monthly & Utility Billing

Idempotent monthly bill generation combining recurring charges (base rent, service charges), variable monthly utilities (electricity, water, gas), and one-time maintenance recoveries with automatic overdue tracking.

Payment Allocation & MFS Ledger

Record Cash, bKash, Nagad, and Bank transfers in under 30 seconds. Supports partial payments, multi-bill settlements, oldest-first bill allocation, and automatic tenant advance credit carry-forward.

Expense Recovery & Fix4U Services

Track property and unit maintenance expenses (plumbing, electrical, repairs, security) with optional tenant bill recovery, paired with the built-in Fix4U smart home and handyman service hub.

Offline SQLite & Encrypted Drive Backup

100% offline-first relational SQLite database ensures zero cloud dependency for daily operations, backed by versioned, password-protected AES-256-GCM encrypted backups to the landlord's Google Drive.

Technical Specifications & System Architecture

Developer Silverbyte Studio (SBS Apps)
Target Audience B2B / Building Landlords, Flat Owners, Residential Property Operators
Application Type Offline-First Property & Rental Management Suite (Android, iOS)
Frontend Framework Flutter (Material 3 Custom Design System)
Language & Architecture Dart 3, Riverpod State Management, go_router, Feature-First Layered Repository Pattern
Database & Persistence SQLite + Drift ORM (Relational Tables, Foreign Keys, UUIDs, Schema Migrations)
Cloud Backup & Encryption Google Drive API v3 + Local AES-256-GCM Encryption & flutter_secure_storage
Reminders & Payments flutter_local_notifications (Offline Due & Agreement Expiry Alerts), Cash / bKash / Nagad / Bank Ledger
